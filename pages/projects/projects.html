Pillar One

Agricultural Wealth Creation

25,000+seedlings

Coffee Seedling Programs

Coffee is the backbone of household income across Greater Masaka. Seedling drives place quality planting material directly into the hands of smallholder farmers — expanding gardens, replacing ageing stock, and building a stronger future harvest.

  • Distribution of quality coffee seedlings to registered farmer groups
  • Guidance on planting, spacing, and garden management
  • Focus on households expanding or renewing their coffee gardens
1,500+sprayers

Agricultural Equipment Distribution

Modern inputs make the difference between a struggling garden and a productive one. Crop sprayers and practical farming equipment are delivered to groups that need them, helping farmers protect their crops and improve yields.

  • Crop sprayers delivered to farmer cooperatives and associations
  • Practical tools that reduce labour and protect harvests
  • Support prioritised for organised, active community groups
Pillar Two

Youth & Women Economic Empowerment

200+youth groups

Youth Empowerment

Young people carry the region's future. Mobilising youth into organised groups opens the door to skills, savings, and enterprise — turning energy and ambition into real local employment and income.

  • Organising youth into cooperatives and savings groups
  • Connecting members to enterprise and skilling opportunities
  • Mentorship toward sustainable self-employment
180+women's groups

Women's Economic Groups

Women's associations are among the most effective engines of household development. Supporting these groups strengthens savings culture, small enterprise, and the economic voice of women across the constituency.

  • Strengthening women's savings and enterprise associations
  • Linking groups to inputs, markets, and financial opportunities
  • Backing women-led initiatives that lift whole households
500+groups engaged

PDM & Emyooga Mobilisation

National wealth-creation programs like the Parish Development Model (PDM) and Emyooga only work when communities understand and access them. Mobilisation ensures families know how to participate — and benefit.

  • Community sensitisation on PDM and Emyooga opportunities
  • Helping groups organise, register, and qualify for support
  • Following through so participation becomes real income
Pillar Three

Infrastructure & Education Development

35+schools assisted

Education Initiatives

Every child kept in school is an investment in the region's future. Partnerships with local schools improve facilities, expand access, and support learners so that education becomes a genuine pathway out of poverty.

  • Support for school facilities and learning environments
  • Partnerships that expand access to quality education
  • Encouraging retention and progression for local learners
120+villages reached

Infrastructure Advocacy

Roads, water, and access shape whether a community can thrive. Advocacy channels local infrastructure needs to the right decision-makers — strengthening local economies and connecting people to opportunity.

  • Carrying community infrastructure priorities to decision-makers
  • Advocating for access that strengthens local economies
  • Championing projects that serve future generations
