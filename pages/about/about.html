A Life Rooted in Service to Bukoto Central & Greater Masaka

Oscar Mutebi Francisco — known across the region as Owebyenkulakulana — has spent his life close to the people: walking through gardens with farmers, sitting in village meetings, and turning national opportunity into practical change for ordinary families.

Biography

From the Villages of Masaka to the Service of a Nation

His story begins where his work continues — in the community.

Born and raised within the Greater Masaka region, Oscar Mutebi Francisco grew up understanding both the richness of the land and the daily struggles of the families who work it. Coffee gardens, savings circles, church halls, and school compounds shaped his early sense of what development should look like: practical, people-centred, and shared.

Over the years he has become a trusted mobiliser and bridge-builder — someone communities call on when they need a program explained, a group organised, an input delivered, or a voice carried to the right table. His approach is simple and consistent: show up, listen first, and turn promises into measurable results.

Leadership Philosophy

Development That Reaches the Last Household

Three convictions guide every engagement, outreach, and initiative.

👂

Listen Before Leading

Communities understand their own needs best. Every initiative starts with sitting down, hearing people out, and shaping solutions around real local priorities.

🌱

Turn Policy Into Practice

National programs only matter when families can access and benefit from them. The work is translating opportunity into seedlings, savings, tools, and enterprise.

🤝

Build With, Not For

Lasting change is co-created. Farmers, women's groups, youth leaders, and schools are partners in the work — never passive recipients of it.

When a farmer's income grows, a child stays in school, and a young person starts a business — that is when development stops being a word and becomes a life changed. — Oscar Mutebi Francisco

State House Service

A Connector Between National Programs & Local People

As a State House official and community mobiliser, Oscar Mutebi Francisco serves as a practical link between national empowerment initiatives and the communities they are designed to serve.

His role is to ensure that wealth-creation programs, agricultural support, and enterprise opportunities do not remain distant announcements — but arrive in villages, reach the right groups, and produce visible improvements in household livelihoods.

  • Mobilising communities to understand and access government programs
  • Coordinating delivery of agricultural inputs and equipment
  • Organising savings groups, youth cooperatives, and women's associations
  • Carrying grassroots feedback back to decision-making tables

Roots in Bukoto Central

He Does Not Represent the Community From a Distance — He Is Part of It

Bukoto Central is not a constituency on a map to Oscar Mutebi Francisco; it is home, family, and lifelong relationship. That closeness is his greatest asset — people know him, trust him, and can find him. It is why farmers bring him their harvest challenges, why youth groups seek his guidance, and why women's associations invite him into their planning.
