Driving Community Development, Expanding Opportunity, Transforming Lives

Oscar Mutebi Francisco is dedicated to advancing economic empowerment, agricultural transformation, youth development, and community progress across Bukoto Central and the wider Greater Masaka region.

Through direct engagement with farmers, savings groups, youth leaders, schools, and local communities, he works to ensure that development opportunities translate into real and measurable improvements in people's lives.

Connecting People & Opportunity

Bringing National Opportunities Closer to the People of Bukoto Central & Greater Masaka

Development programs create their greatest impact when ordinary citizens understand them, access them, and benefit from them.

As a State House official and community mobiliser, Oscar Mutebi Francisco works to connect communities with opportunities that support wealth creation, entrepreneurship, agriculture, education, and local economic development.

His mission is to ensure that national empowerment initiatives become practical tools that help families improve their livelihoods and build a more prosperous future.

Our Commitment

Development Rooted in Service, Partnership & Community Action

Meaningful development is achieved when communities actively participate in shaping their future.

Every engagement, outreach activity, and development initiative is guided by a commitment to listening, collaboration, and practical action that delivers measurable results for local communities.

Together, government, local leadership, community groups, and citizens can build stronger communities and create opportunities that benefit generations to come.
