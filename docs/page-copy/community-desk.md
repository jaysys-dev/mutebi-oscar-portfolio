## We're Listening

*(eyebrow)*

### The Community Desk Is Open to You

This is your direct line to the team. Whether you represent a farmer group, a youth cooperative, a women's association, or a school — or simply have an idea to improve your community — we want to hear from you.

## Community Engagement

*(eyebrow)*

### Every Request Is a Conversation, Not a Form

The Community Desk exists to make sure no request goes unheard. When you reach out, a real person reviews your message, understands your situation, and connects you to the right support — whether that is seedlings, equipment, group organisation, savings guidance, school partnership, or simply an answer to your question.

## How Can We Help?

*(eyebrow)*

### Choose the Kind of Support You Need

Select a request type below, then use the form to tell us more.

#### Farmer Assistance Requests

Coffee seedlings, crop sprayers, farming inputs, or agricultural guidance for your farmer group.

#### Youth Group Requests

Help organising a youth cooperative or savings group, or connecting to enterprise opportunities.

#### Women's Group Requests

Support for women's associations pursuing savings, enterprise, and economic empowerment.

#### School Partnership Requests

Partnership on school facilities, learning access, and initiatives that keep children in school.

#### Community Development Suggestions

Have an idea to improve your village or the wider region? Infrastructure needs, program ideas, or feedback — every suggestion is welcome and reviewed.
